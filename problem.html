THE PROBLEM

The problem is that, in many agricultural markets in Plateau State,
especially in developing regions, farmers:

  • Sell their produce without knowing current market prices
  • Rely on middlemen who reduce their profit
  • Lack access to real-time price information and
  • Experience income instability due to price fluctuations.

This leads to low profit, food waste and poverty in farming communities.
